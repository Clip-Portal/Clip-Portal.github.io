Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

autoclips is a private, single-user desktop application. It has exactly one user: the person who installed it, who is also its author. It is not a public service, it has no sign-up or accounts, and it does not collect information about anyone else, because there is no one else.

What information it accesses

When you connect a YouTube channel, the app requests two permissions through the YouTube Data API v3:

  • Upload videos — to publish clips to your own channel.
  • Manage your videos — to remove an upload again if it was a mistake.

Through those permissions the app can see your channel's name, ID and public statistics, and the videos it uploaded itself. It does not read your private messages, your subscribers, your analytics, or anything else.

Where that information goes

Nowhere. The app runs entirely on your own computer. It does not have a server, a backend, a database in the cloud, or any analytics.

  • Your OAuth credentials are stored locally on your own machine, and are sent only to Google, to authenticate your own requests.
  • Nothing is transmitted to the author of this application, or to any other third party.
  • No data is sold, shared, rented, or used for advertising. There is no advertising.

When selecting moments to clip, the app sends video transcript text to a language model provider to choose highlights. That text is the spoken content of the video being clipped, and it is the only thing sent. No Google user data is included in those requests.

Google API Limited Use

autoclips' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

You are in control of everything, because everything is on your machine.

  • Revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. This immediately invalidates the credentials the app holds.
  • Delete stored credentials by removing the app's local database file (autoclips.db) from its installation folder. They are not copied anywhere else.
  • Delete uploaded videos from YouTube Studio, or with the app's own removal command.

Children

This application is not directed at children and is not distributed to the public. It has no users other than its author.
