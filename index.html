autoclips

A personal tool for turning long videos into short vertical clips.

autoclips is a private, single-user desktop application. It is not a public service, it has no sign-up, and it is not offered to other people. It exists to help its owner produce short-form video from long-form sources.

What it does

  • Transcribes a long video and asks a language model which moments stand alone as clips.
  • Downloads only the chosen segments, rather than the whole source.
  • Generates word-level captions and reframes the video to a vertical 9:16 format.
  • Renders finished clips for review, and uploads the approved ones to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Its YouTube integration

The app connects to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API v3 for one purpose: uploading video the owner has made. It requests two permissions — to upload videos, and to remove them again. It does not read, modify, or delete anything else on the channel.

Every upload passes a human review step first, and uploads are created as private by default. Nothing becomes publicly visible without the owner explicitly choosing so.

Data handling

The app stores its OAuth credentials locally on the owner's own computer. Those credentials are never transmitted anywhere except to Google, to authenticate the owner's own requests. No data is collected from, or about, anybody else. See the Privacy Policy for the details.

This project is unverified with Google and is intended solely for its author's own use. If you have reached it looking for a service to sign up for, there isn't one.
